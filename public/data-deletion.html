User Data Deletion Instructions

Vyaktimetrics (Adsgoat Private Limited) respects your data privacy. If you wish to remove your user activities or delete your data associated with our Facebook integration, you can do so by following these simple steps:

Step-by-Step Deletion Guide

  1. Go to your Facebook Account's Settings & Privacy, and then click on Settings.
  2. Scroll down and click on Apps and Websites on the left-hand menu.
  3. Locate Adsgoat Private Limited or Vyaktimetrics in the list of active apps.
  4. Click the Remove button next to the app name.
  5. To request that we permanently delete any historical dashboard data and cache stored on our servers, please send a direct deletion request to our support team at connect@adsgoat.in.

Once we receive your email, your associated database records, credentials, and stored reports will be completely and permanently removed from our secure systems within 5 business days.
